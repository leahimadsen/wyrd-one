The Wyrd Mandala

Two zodiacs turning against each other. Inside, the sidereal ring: twelve signs, twenty-seven mansions, a hundred and eight padas, and a card of the Buddhist deck for each, where things come from. Outside, the tropical ring with the Nordic deck: where they land. The distance between the rings is the ayanamsa, and the year slider turns it.

Tap any of the 108 stations to open its record: the mansion, both cards, and every system mapped onto it, each marked with how it is held.

Learning the Mandala

The whole instrument is here, free, to study for as long as you like. Learning to work with it is its own course: how to read a station through its rings, how the two rotors and their decks speak to each other, and how to lay your own chart on the wheel and read what lines up.

The course is coming. It will be sold on its own, apart from The Wyrd Reading, and included for the Order.
