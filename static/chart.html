Your card

Which card of the Wyrd deck does my Moon draw?

The Wyrd deck has 108 cards, laid on the Mandala: the Buddhist deck's 36 on the sidereal ring, where things come from; the Nordic deck's 36 on the tropical ring, where they land; and the 36 Asura voids, the tunnels beneath the mansions. Your Moon draws the card of the decan it was born in, read on both rings, and the void it sits over if it sits over one. Share it, or keep it.

The Wyrd Mandala

Where does each of my planets stand among the 108 stations, and what does each mansion hold?

Two rings turning against each other. Inside, the sidereal zodiac cut into its 108 padas, with the Buddhist deck: where things come from. Outside, the tropical zodiac with the Nordic deck: where they land. The gap between them is the ayanamsa, and the year slider turns it. Your planets stand in the middle at their places. Tap any station to open its record, its mansion and every correspondence.
