The Wyrd Mandala

One hundred and eight stations of the sidereal zodiac, each addressable, with the systems mapped onto them held apart and compared rather than equated.

Two rotors · the sidereal ring emanates within, the tropical ring receives without · 0° sidereal Aries at the zenith · 27 × 4 = 108 padas of 3°20′

This is an investigative model. A correspondence shown here is a mapping or a structural relationship under study; it never states that one tradition's figure is another's. Every entry carries its provenance, and a station with no supplied record shows what is missing.

Station 001 is the worked example, drawn from The First Gate. Each mansion carries its short text from the Wyrd corpus. The cards follow The Völva's ruling: the Buddhist deck on the sidereal ring, the Nordic deck on the true tropical ring, both reading the same 36 decans, with The Fool at the gate. The glyphs on the card ring are simple drawn emblems (number, suit, pada) until The Völva's artwork replaces them.
